# swcode 0.15.1

Released 2026-11-17.

**The Android app ships with this release.** v0.15.0's release had no APK, because the notifier asked Android for its notification permission in a way Capacitor 8 refuses, and its foreground service named a notification channel without importing it. Both are fixed: `swcode-android.apk` and its checksum come with this release, and the app offers it as its own next update.

Nothing else changed: the workflows, the settings tabs and the notifications are as 0.15.0 described.
